The live-in flip · the long game

Live in it for five years. Let time do the rest.

This is not a quick turn. The plan is to buy 323 East Street, make it home, do the work while living there, and sell in about five years. The renovation adds value, and so does the time spent living in it.

Tax note. Under IRS rules, if you own and live in the house as your main home for at least 2 of the 5 years before you sell, you may be able to exclude up to $250,000 of gain from income ($500,000 for a married couple filing jointly). A five-year live-in plan is built around this. Confirm your own situation with a tax professional; this is not tax advice.

Recent pace for context: Zillow shows Milford home values up 4.2% over the year through September 30, 2026. FHFA reported Michigan prices up 5.5% from Q4 2024 to Q4 2025, and 4.5% for the East North Central region from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026. Past growth is not a promise: values can be flat or fall. The projection leaves out the costs of owning while you live there (mortgage interest, property taxes, insurance, upkeep), which you would largely be paying for housing anyway.

The path forward

Commit in stages. Resolve the big unknowns first.

01

Before an offer is binding

Confirm measured area, active wiring, insurability and inspection terms. Get local agent feedback on resale range.

02

Before renovation spending

Written electrical, major-system and basement-finishing quotes. Finalize permits and lock a scope that fits the reserve.

03

First year

Electrical and essentials first, then the rest in phases: kitchen, basement, porch and exterior, patio last. Keep permits, receipts and before/after records.

04

Around year five

After living there about five years, refresh comps, confirm the 2-of-5-year tax rule with a tax professional, recalculate selling costs, then set the list price.

Verify before committing
  • Electrical: reported knob-and-tube wiring. Confirm active circuits, panel capacity, scope and insurability.
  • Size: 1,695 sq ft above grade plus an unfinished lower level, against the advertised 2,492. Reconcile before pricing.
  • Structure and systems: roof, foundation, moisture, boiler, plumbing, sewer.
  • Basement: confirm it stays dry before finishing, check ceiling height, and keep the boiler, water heater and shutoffs accessible. A bedroom down there would need a code egress window.
  • Permits: confirm any historic review before exterior changes.
  • Margin is thin at the working case: $4K–$14K at $425K resale. Cost control is the project.
